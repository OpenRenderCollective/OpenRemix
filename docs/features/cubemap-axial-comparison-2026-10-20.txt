Cubemap axial projection comparison (2026-10-20)

Question
Can the six axial first-solid distances already used for capture-site ranking
replace whole-volume fitting and recover the user's authored room bounds?

Method
Use the verified identity-aligned hls00amrl BSP and all 14 combined probe volume
origins from hls2_c1p0.vmap. Traverse BSP segment intervals near-first, using the
same solid/outside-leaf policy as sites.cpp. Each ray is capped at 64 metres,
matching site assessment. Form one box from the six distances. Compare its
intersection-over-union with the authored box against the largest current
balanced fit. This measures geometric agreement only, not reflection quality.
No map or extracted Valve content is committed.

Results
Mean box IoU: current balanced fit 0.222870; axial estimate 0.289612.
Twelve sites improve and two regress (comparison tolerance 0.0001).
The modest aggregate improvement hides large errors. At site 11, the positive-Y
face overshoots the authored face by 2139 Source units; at site 8, positive Y is
2447.7 units beyond the authored face. A doorway or open axial direction does
not provide a room boundary. Site 13 regresses from 0.463 to 0.294 IoU.
The authored volume is a reference, not a claim of perfect geometric room truth.

Decision
Do not replace production fitting with this six-ray box or enlarge receiver
influence to match it. No renderer path, setting, cache revision or rebake is
introduced. Preserve the separate projection/influence representation. The next
geometry experiment must demonstrate both room enclosure and receiver visibility
across doorways/walls; aggregate box volume or mean IoU alone is insufficient.
A bounded candidate must compete with the existing fit, retain authored priority,
and show real Construct receiver coverage gain before changing the default.
