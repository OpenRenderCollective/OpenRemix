Next engineering task
Investigate bounded per-tile/per-cluster probe assignment with a larger map probe
set, or bounded local residency, before increasing a global per-fragment scan.
The current small global atlas/header and selection budget must be treated as an
integration constraint. Keep atlas memory, bake rays, cache migration and shader
work explicit; measure steady-state GPU costs and doorway/fade behavior. Preserve
authored priorities and independent capture points. This can address small rooms
losing to large distant volumes without forcing particular map coordinates into
selection. Compare against a receiver-aware fixed-budget policy before selecting
the final design. Existing automatic enclosure/overlap quality remains open too.
