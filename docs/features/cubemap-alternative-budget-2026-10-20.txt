Cubemap alternative audit budget investigation - 2026-10-20

Question
Are useful room/corridor projection shapes discarded before geometry assessment
because the bounded audit fills spare slots in input-origin order?

Baseline
Current Construct fit/audit uses144boxes from129unique origins. The15alternative
slots come from the first10origins with alternatives. A saved CPU fixture exposes
more shapes, but its fitting bounds/parameters are not an exact runtime substitute;
use actual GPU-audited runtime comparisons for the conclusions below.

Trial1: spread spare slots using the existing site-spreading policy
The same144box/129origin budget audits alternatives across more of the map.
None of the newly audited alternatives enters the final16captures. The old
selected alternative143is displaced by primary53. Equal-area planar brush-surface
coverage inside the common candidate domain falls53.425->52.628percent at16samples
per triangle and53.511->52.688percent at64samples. This surrogate excludes sky,
nodraw and displacements, uses0.05m receiver margin and is not reflection quality
or visibility validation. The regression and lack of a selected new alternative
do not justify retaining the change. Removed the runtime scheduling trial.

Trial2: assess all fitted shapes while separating origin and record budgets
The audit already reuses identical origins' first-hit rays. A temporary prototype
kept the existing160unique-origin cap, allowed at most416CPU shape records
(128primary fits with up to3shapes each plus32grid fallbacks) and retained every
available alternative. Actual Construct produces208boxes from129origins. Both
baseline and prototype trace exactly49,536rays. Every original candidate identity
is preserved as an unchanged prefix. Final16capture identities are identical,
and the final screenshot is pixel-identical. The omitted horizontal corridor
shape at origin[-1312,-2488,-192] gets agreement0.72076 rather than its primary's
0.66976, but still is not selected. Auditing it does not solve coverage.

Both cold runs complete successfully (baseline32.266s, full-shape32.109s); these
single observations are not a bake-performance claim. Diagnostic configuration:
1080p MSAA4,4Mtexels,1024pages,64samples,1bounce,16size32captures,SSRoff,
manual exposure1,cache disabled. This is a geometry-selection experiment, not
normal-quality full renderer acceptance. The prototype's geometry/budget and room
fitting suites pass, including shared-origin ray counts and independent caps.

Decision
Remove both runtime trials, new budget constants and prototype-only assertions.
No cache revision, dependency, option, new retained rendering path or deployment.
The installed direct-style-occupancy package stays current. More alternatives and
spare-slot redistribution are not the next useful change under the present fitting
and scoring policy. Do not repeat these experiments without a new fitting or
receiver-quality objective. Investigate receiver-local projection error and
wall/doorway influence coverage instead of trying more arbitrary volume weights.
The broader cubemap, performance and lighting delivery gates remain open.
