Independent denoiser queue selection - 2026-10-20

Issue and change
The four-queue CPU trial was conditional on serial CPU first beating the ordinary
CUDA device by 35 percent. This excluded workloads where parallel CPU wins but
serial CPU narrowly misses that margin. Eligible chart-heavy workloads on machines
with at least 16 logical processors now measure the parallel candidate independently.
Selection still requires parallel CPU to beat ordinary device by 35 percent and
serial CPU by 25 percent. Explicit device/thread overrides, bounded copied packets,
error fallback, cancellation and disjoint chart ownership are unchanged. No new
setting, cache format or cache-key change. The native policy is engine agnostic.
