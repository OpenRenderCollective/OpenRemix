Placed sky-prop clipping checkpoint - 2026-10-20

Outcome
The Construct white-room tree-shaped intrusion was geometry, not a lightmap or
reflection artifact. The Source adapter clipped sky world polygons against
playable BSP space, but tested only each sky prop's origin. Large model cards
could therefore extend into the playable room from an outside origin.

Wrapper commit a1b11bf clips placed prop triangles using the same BSP partition,
then restores model-space positions/normals and interpolated vertex attributes.
Unchanged triangles retain exact attributes. Material/replacement lookup and
placement transforms are preserved. Per-placement mesh ownership and destruction
are explicit; temporary full vertex attributes are released after map loading.
This Source-specific policy remains in the adapter, not the native renderer.

Evidence
All 16 wrapper tests pass, including rotated/nonuniform placements, winding,
interpolation, unchanged/removed geometry and malformed input handling.
Construct retains all 157 sky props; three placements need clipped meshes and
no models fail loading. The large tree intrusion disappears in unlit and shaded
captures. Compared with disabling sky geometry, only two boundary pixels remain
different (maximum nine byte levels), versus 8,488 pixels before the fix.
An outside capture confirms foliage remains enabled.

A fresh Construct bake and two warm reloads have identical published content
hashes and pixel-identical shaded images. Changed actual geometry correctly
changes the bake input hash to 15365796088336057046 and cache key to
18198587294272886556. Crossfire retains its previous cache and renders pixel-
identically to the prior native checkpoint. Vulkan validation and synchronization
checks report no validation errors or synchronization hazards in these runs.
Fresh-bake timings were collected with validation enabled and are not comparable
with earlier performance measurements; no bake-speed improvement is claimed.

Limits and next work

replacements are not re-clipped after native replacement resolution; preservation
of lookup identity does not establish replacement-geometry parity. This fix does
