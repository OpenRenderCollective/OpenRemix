Conservative chart/style occupancy - 2026-10-20

Change
Grouped direct composition now marks the atlas tiles intersecting captured chart
rectangles owned by the current controllable light group. All other tiles are
known black for that group and use the streaming builder's existing skip hook.
The entire rectangle is retained, including captured seam donors. Marking uses
immutable chart/light ownership, not camera culling or light visibility guesses.
Missing capture/probe coverage still declines the whole group before composition.
Nonempty tile sampling, light order, signed SH, quantization and probes are unchanged.

One byte per atlas tile is temporary for one group at a time:16KiB for Construct's
four1024pages, at most16MiB within existing dimension limits. No global state,
retained bake/GPU allocation, shader, new setting or cache-key/format change.
All chart rectangles and owner identities retain their existing validation; skip
traversal retains cancellation and all-or-nothing budget behavior.
