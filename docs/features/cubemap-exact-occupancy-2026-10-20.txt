Exact occupancy diagnostic for authored cubemap bounds - 2026-10-20

Question
Does conservative AABB clipping through oblique BSP planes explain why the14
aligned hls00 authored combined-volume boxes fail current occupancy policy?

Method
Offline diagnostic carries the complete signed root-to-leaf plane path for each
solid/outside fragment reached by the production-style AABB traversal. For each
fragment, a normalized-plane linear program finds its largest inscribed sphere
inside the query box. Positive-interior intersections are reconstructed from all
halfspaces and measured by convex hull volume. Query coordinates use float32,
matching the native fixture input. No runtime fitter or cache identity changed.
SciPy1.15.3 performs the offline LP/intersection/hull work; this is not proposed as
a runtime dependency or a new user-facing tool.

An independent seeded100,000-point BSP classification per authored box checks the
volume sums without using fragment hulls. All14 agree within1.64standard errors.
This validates the partition-volume calculation, not the original author's intent
or exact physical/visual parity between the edited Source2 port and Source1 BSP.

Exact clipping materially reduces some volume estimates (notably index3), but13
of14boxes still exceed the current8percent ignored-volume allowance. Therefore
exact clipping alone cannot admit those13boxes under the established policy.
Index12 has only1.20percent exact solid/outside volume but includes broad boundary
fragments that fail the existing two-thin/interior-axis detail classifier. Low
volume alone does not establish that relaxing that classifier is safe.

Important interpretation
Negative-cluster leaves mean solid OR outside/nonplayable partition space here;
these fractions are not a measurement of visible solid mesh volume. They do not
show the hand-authored boxes are wrong. Authoring boxes can intentionally enclose
walls, trim or nonplayable space. Previously verified common geometry/alignment
also does not prove that every surface of the edited port matches this BSP.
This is geometric evidence, not a rendered quality or performance acceptance test.

Decision and next work
Do not replace conservative fitting with an expensive exact polyhedron solver on
the assumption it fixes coverage; the reference comparison contradicts that.
Do not arbitrarily increase allowed solid volume to match these boxes. Keep this
checkpoint diagnostic-only. The useful next fit must distinguish enclosure used
for parallax projection from where actual room receivers can safely blend, and
must check wall-separated receivers/doorway coverage rather than whole-box empty
volume alone. Test proposed receiver-domain representation on Construct and the
aligned reference before adding runtime searches or relaxing bounds.
