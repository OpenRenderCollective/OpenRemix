Separate geometry artifact
The white-room image contains a tree-shaped patch against the wall at upper right.
It remains in albedo-only view. Two additional albedo/no-bake controls differ only
in OPENREMIX_PROXY_SKY_TRACE=1/0. Disabling the merged sky geometry removes the
patch: 8488 changed pixels, bounding rectangle [1822,385,1919,550], max channel delta 161.
Both controls were visually inspected. These unlit/no-bake controls are diagnostic,
not performance comparisons and not settings deployed to the game.
This localizes that artifact to merged 3D-skybox geometry, not lighting/reflections.
The wrapper clips transformed sky world polygons against playable BSP regions;
static sky props currently test only their transformed origin before keeping the
whole model. The exact offending prop/triangle and safe clipping fix remain to be
established. Do not disable sky geometry as a product workaround or enlarge
cubemap bounds to address this tree. This does not explain all earlier ceiling
lighting artifacts reported by the user.
